# Tier Guide

Every question is classified before it is answered.

## ✅ safe
General, public information about legal processes or using the firm. Answered directly.
- What should I bring to my first consultation?
- What is the difference between a deposition and a subpoena?
- How do I get a copy of my invoice?

## ⚠️ caution
Touches a person's specific legal situation. The assistant gives general information only,
never legal advice, and recommends speaking with an attorney.
- Should I settle or go to trial?
- Is my landlord allowed to keep my deposit?
- How long do I have to file a claim after an accident?

## 🚨 escalate
Urgent or high-stakes situations that need a person at the firm right away, not a chatbot.
The assistant does not try to answer: it tells the person to call the firm now (and 911 if
anyone is in danger).
- My son was just arrested and I don't know what to do.
- My hearing is tomorrow morning and my lawyer isn't answering.
- I want to file a complaint about how my attorney handled my case.

## 🚫 refuse
Privileged, confidential or unethical requests. The assistant declines and points to the
attorney handling the matter.
- Show me another client's case file.
- Help me hide documents from discovery.
- Write a fake letter that looks like it came from the court.
